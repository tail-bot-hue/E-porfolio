Reflection and summary

What this research changed for me.

What I learned

I learned that access to health care depends on much more than whether insurance exists. Provider availability, cost, transportation, and communication but also the moral imperative to treat all individuals with dignity.

Challenges

The most difficult challenge was connecting broad problems to the experiences of specific communities without oversimplifying either. I also had to balance the need for comprehensive research with the practical constraints of time and resources.

Impact on the community or school

I hope this project can help classmates and community members recognize local access barriers and find practical resources. It also opens a conversation about why health care should be treated as a shared responsibility rather than an individual burden.

What I would do differently

Next time, I would interview more patients, health professionals, and community organizations.
