My volunteering Letter

Dear community partner,

My name is Tai Le, and I am a senior at John Paul II Catholic School, and I am writing to express my interest in volunteering with your organization. At John Paul II service is a large component of our school; in fact it is one of our core values. Prior to our senior year we have been required to complete community service hours and each year we participate in a school wide day of service. Each senior is required to complete a capstone project that encourages us to delve into current social issues. As part of our capstone experience, we are required to create and execute a community service project that is in partnership with a local organization. For my capstone experience, I am researching how physician shortages, rising healthcare costs, and systemic barriers affect access to care in the U.S., and how Catholic social teaching can promote policies that ensure affordable, accessible health care for all. I feel that your organization would help me better understand this issue and how organizations are working to lessen this problem. In my own free time, I would like to work behind the scenes at the hospital to help lessen the burden of mundane tasks. I am also interested in pursuing a career in the medical field so I would appreciate it if I could interact with the doctors and medical personnel . I would appreciate an opportunity to meet with you and your organization to determine how I can be of service to your organization. I can be reached at tai.le@thejp2.org
Thank you very much for your time and attention in this matter.

Sincerely,
Tai Le
