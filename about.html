About the researcher

My name is Tai Le, and I am a senior high school student at John Paul II Catholic High School exploring the relationship between health care policy, ethics, and community well-being.

Purpose

This e-portfolio presents my research on how physician shortages, rising costs, and systemic barriers affect access to care in the United States. It also considers how Catholic social teaching can help guide policies that protect human dignity and serve the common good.
